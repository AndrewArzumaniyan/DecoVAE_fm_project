{\fontsize{13.5}{18}\selectfont\color{teal}\bfseries Forecasting task\par}
\vspace{6pt}
{\fontsize{14}{20}\selectfont
$\mathbf{x}=(x_{t-T+1},\ldots,x_t)\in\mathbb{R}^{T}$\par
$\mathbf{y}=(x_{t+1},\ldots,x_{t+H})\in\mathbb{R}^{H}$\par}
\vspace{3pt}
{\color{muted}$T$: context length; $H$: forecast horizon.\par}
\vspace{7pt}
{\fontsize{15}{21}\selectfont
$\displaystyle \mathbf{x}\longmapsto p_\theta(\mathbf{y}\mid\mathbf{x})$\par}
\vspace{7pt}
{\color{muted}Quantiles and intervals describe forecast uncertainty.\par}
\vspace{8pt}
{\color{muted}\textbf{Example:} 96 past hours, a 24-hour forecast.\par}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.48\textwidth}
\raggedright
{\fontsize{13.5}{18}\selectfont\color{teal}\bfseries How DecoVAE-FM represents uncertainty\par}
\vspace{6pt}
Trend and seasonal outputs form the mean. Residual features also inform the variance.\par
\vspace{7pt}
{\fontsize{15}{21}\selectfont
$\displaystyle \mu=\tau+s,\qquad \log\sigma^2=g_\theta(\mathbf{z})$\par}
\vspace{7pt}
{\color{muted}
$\tau,s$: trend / seasonal forecasts.\par
\vspace{5pt}
$\mathbf{z}$ collects learned feature vectors of the trend, seasonal and residual components, obtained through their encoders.\par}
\vspace{8pt}
{\color{muted}Mean and variance are in normalized model space.\par}
